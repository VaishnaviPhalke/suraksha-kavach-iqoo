# Suraksha Kavach — 60–90s Demonstration & Presentation Script

This guide provides a structured presenter script and timing breakdown for recording a 60–90 second hackathon or stakeholder demonstration video.

---

### ⏱️ Timing & Stage Breakdown

#### [0:00 - 0:20] Introduction & Airplane Mode Verification
- **Visual**: Show the Android device operating in **Airplane Mode** (airplane icon active in status bar).
- **Audio / Script**:
  > *"When dealing with confidential schematics, diplomatic cables, or proprietary AI models, traditional cloud storage represents a severe attack vector. Meet **Suraksha Kavach**—an enterprise security utility that is 100% air-gapped. Notice the persistent 'Air-Gapped | 0 Outbound Requests' badge. `android.permission.INTERNET` is completely excluded at the operating system level."*

#### [0:20 - 0:40] Hardware-Encrypted Vault (AES-256-GCM)
- **Visual**: On Screen 1 ("My Shield"), tap the key icon on `defense_cad_blueprints.enc` to decrypt it in volatile RAM; tap "Lock File" to create a new encrypted file.
- **Audio / Script**:
  > *"All files in the vault are protected by AES-256-GCM bound directly to the Android Hardware KeyStore. Keys never leave the device's Secure Element. When an authorized operator unlocks a file, decryption occurs solely in volatile memory—leaving zero plaintext footprint on disk."*

#### [0:40 - 1:05] 3-Factor Multi-Modal Gate & AI Threat Sentinel
- **Visual**: Switch to Screen 2 (Access Gate) and trigger the 3 checks. Switch to Screen 3 (Sentinel), tap the speaker icon for audible TTS alert.
- **Audio / Script**:
  > *"Access requires passing three independent factors: an optical asymmetric QR scan via CameraX, a StrongBox biometric fingerprint check, and a localized Haversine GPS geo-fence verifying you are within the 50-meter bunker zone.
  > Simultaneously, the Snapdragon NPU runs on-device localized reasoning to evaluate a real-time risk score of 0.02. If anomalous access occurs, the hands-free voice sentinel triggers an audible challenge: 'Unusual access detected. Confirm or Revoke?'"*

#### [1:05 - 1:30] iQOO Office Kit Sentinel Tunnel (Laptop Console)
- **Visual**: Switch to Screen 4 (Sync Bridge). Show the laptop screen displaying the Sentinel Console on `http://localhost:3000` via USB 3.2.
- **Audio / Script**:
  > *"For desk operations, the iQOO Office Kit Sentinel Tunnel establishes a high-speed, hardware-isolated USB 3.2 link transferring live telemetry at 480 Mbps straight to our laptop console without ever touching the internet. Every event is sealed in a cryptographic SHA-256 hash-chain SQLite ledger for tamper-proof compliance."*
